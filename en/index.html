How it works

From one sentence to a finished video.

Claude follows the skill’s process and a local rendering engine handles everything that must not be improvised: timing, figures, maps and sound.

  1. 01

    You ask

    In plain language. With a topic, or with your own material: a text, your notes, a document or your data.

What’s inside

Everything a video needs to work.

Stories with a method

Stakes, big question, twist and re-hook. If the script fails a ten-criterion self-review, it gets rewritten.

Facts with sources

Every figure, date and name is cross-checked against at least two sources. Anything doubtful is flagged or dropped.

Natural HD voice

Continuous narration with an HD neural voice. Free on Azure’s free tier, or with your own voice.

Everything in sync

Every element appears and sounds exactly when the voice names it. Word-by-word captions.

Exact maps and charts

Bars, lines, counters and maps of the world, Europe or Spain, with World Bank and Our World in Data figures.

Five styles, your brand

Paper, neon, pop, chalkboard and clean, adaptable to your colours.

Storyboard included

Scene by scene: timing, narration, purpose, visual, camera, sound and text.

Learns from your metrics

Reads screenshots of your stats and writes your own style guide for the next videos.

Measured, not assumed

Every release incorporates what the data says.

We publish daily with the skill itself and track the retention of every video on every network. What we learn ships in the next release as rules for scripts, sound and style.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use it to explain my project, lesson or company?

Yes. Instead of looking for a fun fact, you give it your material (a text, your notes, a document or your data) and it turns it into a story with a hook, a question and a payoff. The figures on screen are yours.

Does it use the Claude API?

No. It runs inside Claude Code on your subscription (Pro or Max). Each video uses part of your plan’s usage limit, not a separate bill.

How long does a video take?

15 to 30 minutes from the request, mostly research and drawing. Rendering itself takes 2-3 minutes on an ordinary laptop.

How many videos can I make?

On the Pro plan, usually 1-2 a day. On Max, considerably more.

Are the facts reliable?

Every figure is cross-checked against at least two sources, uncertain claims are flagged, and sources are listed in the post copy. Still, review each video before publishing.

What language is the narration in?

Spanish, for now.

Do I need to code?

No. The installer handles dependencies, and everything else is requested in plain language.

Does it publish automatically?

No. The skill delivers the files; you decide where and when to post them.
