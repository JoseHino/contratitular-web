How it works

Six stages, one request.

You ask for a topic in plain language. Claude follows the skill's process and uses a local rendering engine for everything that must not be improvised: timing, figures, maps and sound.

  1. 01

    Research

    Web search and cross-checking of every figure, date and name against at least two sources. Anything uncertain is flagged as a hypothesis or dropped.

  2. 02

    Story

    Stakes, big question, twist and rehook. A ten-criterion self-review: if the average drops below 7.5, the script is rewritten.

  3. 03

    Script & storyboard

    Continuous narration at ~3 words per second, fitted to the requested length. Camera, transition and sound per scene.

  4. 04

    Scenes

    Vector illustration drawn for every line. Charts and maps built from real data (World Bank, Our World in Data, Natural Earth, IGN).

  5. 05

    Voice & sound

    Neural voice, word-level alignment and procedurally generated sound effects that play when each element is mentioned.

  6. 06

    Render

    Local Remotion: H.264 MP4, normalised loudness, cover image, post copy with sources, and a storyboard.

FAQ

Does it use the Claude API?

No. It runs inside Claude Code on your subscription (Pro or Max). Each video uses part of your plan's usage limit, not a separate bill.

How long does a video take?

15 to 30 minutes from the request, mostly research and drawing. Rendering itself takes 2-3 minutes on an ordinary laptop.

How many videos can I make?

On the Pro plan, usually 1-2 a day. On Max, considerably more.

Are the facts reliable?

Every figure is cross-checked against at least two sources, uncertain claims are flagged, and sources are listed in the post copy. Still, review each video before publishing.

Does it publish automatically?

No. The skill delivers the files; you decide where and when to post them.

Do I need to code?

No. The installer handles dependencies, and everything else is requested in plain language.
