How it works

From one sentence to a finished video.

No filming, no editing software and no technical knowledge.

  1. 01

    You ask

    In your own words, as you would ask a person. The topic is enough, or give it your notes, a document or your data.

  2. 02

    It makes itself

    It finds the information, writes the story, draws and animates every scene and adds voice and captions.

  3. 03

    You post it

    You get the finished video, with its cover and the text to go with it. You decide where and when.
