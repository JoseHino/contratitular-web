FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I really pay nothing per video?

Correct. Claude writes the script and draws the scenes with your paid plan, the voice-over comes from Microsoft Azure's free tier and the editing runs on your computer. The only thing you pay for is your Claude subscription, and each video counts towards its usage limit.

How many videos can I make a day?

On the Pro plan, usually 1 or 2 a day. On a Max plan, quite a few more. Each video uses a handful of requests to Claude.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You follow the guide, Claude installs what's needed, and then you just ask for videos in plain language.

Are the facts reliable?

The skill makes Claude cross-check every fact against several sources and do a second pass before drawing. The sources are listed in the post copy. Even so, we recommend taking a look before you post.

Does it post automatically?

No: the skill hands you the video and the copy, and you decide where and when to upload it.

Can I use it for my brand or business?

Yes. You can change the topics, colours and style to suit your account.

Do you guarantee views?

No. We give you the tool to post consistently and with quality; results depend on each account.
